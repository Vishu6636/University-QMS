# Privacy Policy — University Query Management System (UQMS)

**Last updated:** 2 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the University Query Management System ("UQMS", "we", "us", "the platform") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. This platform is operated as part of a pilot deployment for [University Name] and is committed to handling your data responsibly in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

By using UQMS, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

---

### 1. Who This Applies To

This policy applies to all users of UQMS, including:
- Students and staff of the onboarded university ("Registered Users")
- Prospective inquirers using the public query feature ("Prospective Inquirers")
- University administrators managing the platform ("Admins")

---

### 2. What Information We Collect

Depending on your role, we may collect:

| Data Type | Collected From | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | Registered Users, Admins | Identify your queries and tickets |
| **Email address** | Registered Users, Admins | Account access, ticket updates, notifications |
| **Student/Enrollment ID** (if applicable) | Registered Users | Verify affiliation with the university |
| **Query/ticket content** | All users | Provide helpdesk responses via AI and human escalation |
| **Login timestamps and session activity** | All users | Security, fraud prevention, session management |
| **University affiliation** (university_id) | All users | Route your data to the correct tenant only |

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, health records, or biometric data through this platform.

---

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data only to:
- Respond to your queries via the AI assistant or human staff
- Route and prioritize support tickets
- Maintain account security and prevent unauthorized access
- Improve response accuracy (in aggregated, de-identified form where possible)
- Comply with legal obligations, if any arise

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

### 4. AI Processing

Your queries may be processed by third-party AI language model providers (currently Groq, running Llama 3.3) to generate responses. Query text is sent to this provider for the sole purpose of generating a response and is not used by us to train any AI model. Please avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information in your queries beyond what is necessary to resolve your issue.

---

### 5. Data Isolation Between Universities

UQMS is a multi-tenant platform. Your data is strictly scoped to your university (`university_id`) and is technically isolated from other universities using the platform. University Admins can only access data belonging to their own institution.

---

### 6. Data Retention

- Ticket and query data is retained for as long as your account is active, or as required to resolve your query, whichever is longer.
- Inactive accounts and associated data may be deleted after 12 months of inactivity, unless retention is required for legal or dispute-resolution purposes.
- You may request earlier deletion at any time (see Section 8).

---

### 7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical safeguards, including:
- Encrypted password storage (hashing)
- Access controls limiting data visibility to your own university's Admins
- Secure, access-controlled hosting infrastructure

No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

---

### 8. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- Withdraw consent and request deletion of your data, subject to any legal retention requirements
- Raise a grievance regarding how your data is handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: 6vishu66@gmail.com

We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe.

---

### 9. Consent

By creating an account or submitting a query, you consent to the collection and processing of your data as described in this policy. For Prospective Inquirers, submitting a query without an account constitutes consent to process that query solely to generate a response.

---

### 10. Children's Data

UQMS is intended for use by university students, staff, and prospective applicants, generally aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age without appropriate consent as required by applicable law.

---

### 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, particularly as the platform moves from pilot to wider deployment. Material changes will be communicated to Registered Users via the platform or email where possible.
